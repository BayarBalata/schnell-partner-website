# SCHNELL Business and Website Analysis

## Business Summary

SCHNELL Lubricants Germany presents itself as a German-made automotive lubricant, additive and car care brand. Its public positioning is built around speed, precision, performance, strict quality control and global distribution.

The strongest business claims on the current website are:

- SCHNELL means "fast" in German.
- The company was started by Klaus Bosshammer.
- Core products include additives, technical spray-can products and lubricants.
- The brand says it has operations in Germany, Singapore, UAE and Philippines.
- It says it works with distribution partners in more than 80 countries.
- It positions production as Germany-based and aligned with ISO 9001 quality management.
- Contact details list OWS Germany in Kassel and a distributor presence in Duhok, Kurdistan Region, Iraq.

## Website Findings

The existing website uses:

- A black, white and bright green SCHNELL identity.
- Orange accents on some product and footer interface elements.
- Oxanium-style industrial headline typography and Sarabun-style body copy.
- Large hero messaging around "SCHNELL Performance 3 in 1."
- Main navigation: Home, About Schnell, Products and Contact.
- A deep product taxonomy across additives, lubricants, oils, coolants, brake fluids and OEM solutions.

The rebuild keeps the same commercial direction and brand mood, but improves the structure for a modern launch:

- Cleaner first viewport with direct value proposition and calls to action.
- Product filtering by market segment.
- Stronger distributor-ready messaging.
- Responsive mobile navigation.
- Lightweight static hosting with no WordPress dependency.
- Scroll reveal, counter and hover animations without heavy plugins.

## Product Catalog Detail Added

The public SCHNELL product pages were reviewed category by category and converted into a browsable website catalog. The new site section keeps the 16 public families above, then exposes product names under each family where the source pages listed them. Examples include passenger-car engine oils from 0W-16 through 20W-50, ATF/CVT/DSG transmission fluids, commercial truck oils, marine oils, motorcycle oils, coolants, brake fluids, additives and the Performance 3 in 1 service products.

This is presentation-ready structure, but not final product compliance data. Before publishing for sales, replace or verify each item against official SCHNELL TDS/SDS files, packaging sizes, Iraq stock availability and approved Arabic/Kurdish product naming.

The product-family images were downloaded from the public SCHNELL product page into `assets/products/` so the static website does not depend on remote hotlinked images. Individual product-card images were downloaded from the public category pages into `assets/product-items/` and connected to the catalog data in `assets/product-items/product-items.json`. Confirm final image-use approval under the partnership before public launch.
